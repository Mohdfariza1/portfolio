Our Services

Full-scope engineering and construction services — from ground preparation to handover.

Category 01

Civil & Earthworks

We provide complete civil and earthworks services for industrial estates, residential townships, commercial developments, and government infrastructure. Our heavy machinery fleet allows us to mobilise within 48 hours of award.

Site clearing & vegetation removal
Bulk earthworks & cut-and-fill
Slope protection & retaining walls
Drainage & stormwater systems
Subgrade preparation & compaction
Silt trap & erosion control (DOE)
Category 02

Structural Works

From foundation to rooftop, our structural works division handles reinforced concrete construction for commercial, industrial, and government buildings. All works supervised by PE-registered engineers.

Bored & driven piling works
RC pad & raft foundations
Column, beam & slab construction
Pre-stressed & post-tensioned works
Structural steel fabrication & erection
Structural repair & strengthening
Category 03

Road & Infrastructure

Registered under JKR Approved Contractor List, we execute road and infrastructure works for state governments, local authorities, and private developers. QLASSIC-certified with consistent scores above 75.

New road construction (AC pavement)
Road widening & realignment
Bridge construction & replacement
Culverts, box drains & headwalls
Road markings & signage
Rehabilitation & resurfacing works
Category 04

M&E Works

Our M&E division handles full mechanical and electrical installation for commercial and industrial buildings, working alongside or independently from main civil contractors.

HV/LV electrical distribution
Lighting & power wiring
HVAC system design & install
Plumbing & sanitary works
Fire suppression & alarm system
BMS & building automation
Category 05

Building Renovation

Renovation and refurbishment works for occupied and non-occupied buildings. We plan works to minimise disruption and maintain safety throughout construction phases.

Office & commercial space fit-out
Factory layout redesign
Roofing & waterproofing
Wall, ceiling & floor finishes
Government facility upgrading
DBKL / MBPJ compliance works
Category 06

Project Management

For clients who need oversight without self-performing, we offer project management consultancy — from tender evaluation through to practical completion and CCC application.

Programme planning (P6 / MS Project)
QA/QC inspection & NCR management
Weekly progress report to client
Subcontractor management
Variation & claims administration
CCC application & handover
How It Works

Our Process

01

Inquiry & Briefing

Submit your project brief. We respond within 24 hours for initial assessment.

02

Site Visit & Quotation

Our engineer visits site, assesses scope, and issues a detailed BOQ-based quotation.

03

Award & Mobilisation

Contract signed, programme submitted, site established. Mobilise within agreed timeframe.

04

Delivery & Handover

Project completed, inspected, defects cleared. CCC or handover certificate issued.
