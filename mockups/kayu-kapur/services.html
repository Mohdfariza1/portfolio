Services

Four kinds of work, each under one contract with one supervisor.

  • Whole-house renovation

    8 to 12 weeks

    • Wet works, wiring and plumbing
    • Carpentry for every room
    • Paint, limewash and flooring
    • One contract, one supervisor

  • Kitchens

    About 4 weeks

    • Cabinets built in our workshop
    • Stone or solid-surface worktop
    • Sink, hob and hood fitted
    • Fitted on site in two days

  • Bathrooms

    About 3 weeks

    • Stripped to the slab
    • Waterproofing, flood-tested for two days
    • Tile, microcement or limewash
    • Vanity, shower screen and fittings

  • Built-in carpentry

    2 to 3 weeks

    • Wardrobes and TV walls
    • Shoe cabinets and study desks
    • Measured and cut to your walls
    • Soft-close hinges and runners

How we price

  1. Free site visit

    We measure and take photos. No charge in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

  2. A quotation that lists every item

    Each cabinet, each wall, each fitting has its own line and price.

  3. The price is fixed

    It changes only if you add work, and we quote that first.

  4. Paid in four stages

    10% to book, 40% when work starts, 40% when carpentry is in, 10% at handover.

Questions we hear first

How much does a renovation cost?

It depends on the house and the work. After the free site visit you get a quotation that lists every item, and that price is fixed.

How long does it take?

A bathroom takes about three weeks, a kitchen about four, a whole house eight to twelve. Your quotation states the dates.

Do you work in condominiums?

Yes. We handle the management office: the renovation form, the deposit and the working hours.

Is there a warranty?

Twelve months on workmanship, counted from handover.

Which areas do you cover?

Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Subang Jaya, Shah Alam, Klang and the rest of Selangor.
