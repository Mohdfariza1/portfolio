Swapping a size

Free within 14 days, as long as the piece is unworn and unwashed.

  1. Tell us

    Message us your order number and the size you need.

  2. Post it back

    We send a prepaid label. Drop the parcel at any courier counter.

  3. Get the new size

    We post the new size the day your parcel reaches us.

Looking after batik

  • Wash it alone in cold water for the first two washes.
  • Use a mild soap. No bleach, no soaking overnight.
  • Dry it in the shade, inside out, so the colour stays.
  • Iron on the inside at a medium heat.
