Before you order

How do I pick my size?

Measure a shirt you already like, laid flat, and match it to the size table. If it still does not fit, the swap is free within 14 days.

How long does delivery take?

We post within one working day. It takes 2 to 4 days in the Peninsula and 4 to 7 days to Sabah and Sarawak.

Will the colour run?

A little, in the first two washes, as with all hand-dyed batik. Wash it on its own in cold water for those two washes.

Can I pay with FPX?

Yes. FPX from any Malaysian bank, Visa, Mastercard, Touch 'n Go eWallet and GrabPay.

Do you make uniforms for companies?

Yes, from 20 pieces, in your own colour. Send us a message from the contact page.
