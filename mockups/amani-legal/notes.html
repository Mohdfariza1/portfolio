12 September 2026

Three clauses to check in your employment contracts

Most contracts we are shown were copied from somewhere else years ago. These three parts are where they tend to be thin.

  • Probation: how long it runs, and what has to happen for it to end or be extended.
  • Restraint after leaving: Malaysian law treats most of these clauses as void, so protect confidential information instead.
  • Personal data: say what you collect about staff and why, and get their consent in writing.

28 August 2026

Buying a sub-sale unit: read this before paying the booking fee

Money paid before the agreement is signed is harder to get back than most buyers expect.

  • Ask who holds the booking fee, and get the terms for a refund in writing.
  • Check the title: who owns it, and whether the land office or the state must consent to the sale.
  • Have your loan approved in principle before you commit to the dates in the agreement.

14 August 2026

Two brothers, one Sdn Bhd, no shareholders agreement

The commonest gap we see in family businesses. Three decisions are worth writing down while everyone still agrees.

  • Who decides what, and which decisions need both of you.
  • What happens to the shares if one of you wants to leave, or dies.
  • How the company pays you: salary, dividends, and loans to directors.
