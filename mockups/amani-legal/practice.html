Practice areas

Four areas. Each has one partner who answers for it.

Corporate and commercial

Company formation, shareholder agreements, supply contracts, and the paperwork behind the sale of a business.

For owners of Sdn Bhd companies, family businesses and partners starting something together.

  • Shareholders and joint venture agreements
  • Supply and service contracts
  • Sale of shares or of a business
  • Company secretarial matters
Amani ZulkifliManaging partner

Conveyancing and property

Sub-sale and developer purchases, loan documents, tenancy and lease agreements, and strata title problems.

For house buyers and sellers, landlords, and businesses taking a shop or an office.

  • Sale and purchase agreements
  • Loan and charge documents
  • Tenancies and commercial leases
  • Strata and management body disputes
Lim Kok HengPartner, property

Employment

Contracts and handbooks that hold up, and representation at the Labour Department and the Industrial Court.

For employers with a staff problem, and senior employees who have been let go.

  • Employment contracts and handbooks
  • Domestic inquiries
  • Unfair dismissal claims
  • Retrenchment advice
S. RameshPartner, employment

Family and probate

Divorce, custody and maintenance, wills, and the grant of probate or letters of administration after a death.

For families going through a separation, and anyone putting their affairs in order.

  • Divorce and custody
  • Maintenance claims
  • Wills and trusts
  • Probate and administration
Nurul FarhanaPartner, family
