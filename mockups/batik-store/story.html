Our story

We started Kirana because the batik we owned came out once a year. We wanted pieces to wear on an ordinary Monday.

Two workshops, paid by the piece and paid fairly

Our cloth is stamped in one workshop near Kota Bharu and one in Kuala Terengganu. We order small runs, pay on delivery, and never ask for a rush that would cut corners.

The workshops in this demo are made up.

How a piece is made

  1. Draw and cut

    We draw the motif, and a coppersmith shapes it into a stamp.

  2. Stamp with wax

    The stamp goes into hot wax, then onto the cloth, again and again down its length.

  3. Dye and boil

    The cloth is dyed, then boiled so the wax lifts and the pattern shows.

  4. Cut and sew

    It comes to Shah Alam, where we cut and sew it into shirts, kurung and scarves.

Come and try them on

Our studio in Shah Alam is open on weekdays. Every size of every piece is on the rail.
