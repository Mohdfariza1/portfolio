Our approach

How a matter runs with us

Most clients come to us frustrated by not being told what is happening. We fixed that with a fixed rhythm, not a promise.

Start with a consultation
1

First consultation

45 minutes, with the partner who would handle the matter. You leave knowing your options and the likely cost range.

2

Written scope and fee

Before any work starts you receive a letter setting out what we will do, what we will not, and the fee for each stage.

3

Fortnightly updates

A short written update every two weeks even when nothing has moved — so you never have to chase us for status.

4

Closing file and handover

At the end you get the full file, originals, and a plain-language note of anything you need to diarise or renew.
