About Us

Two decades of delivering engineering excellence across Peninsular Malaysia.

Our Story

Founded on Site. Built on Trust.

Cergas Bina Engineering Sdn Bhd was established in 2004 by a group of civil engineers who believed that Malaysia's construction industry needed contractors who were not just builders — but partners in delivery.

Starting with small civil earthworks contracts in Selangor, the company grew steadily through repeat business and referrals. By 2010, we had achieved CIDB Grade G7 — qualifying us for contracts of unlimited value across all categories of construction work.

Today, with over 150 completed projects across Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, Johor, and Pahang, Cergas Bina remains a privately held, engineer-led firm. Every project is personally overseen by our directors.
