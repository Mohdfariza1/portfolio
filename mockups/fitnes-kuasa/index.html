Four ways to train

Every class has a cap, so the coach can correct your form.

  • Strength Club

    Squat, press and deadlift with a barbell. Your coach writes down your numbers each week, so you can see the load go up.

    60 minutes, 12 people at most

  • HIIT Burn

    Short, hard rounds on the rower, the bike and the sled. Book this one if you have 45 minutes and want to sweat.

    45 minutes, 16 people at most

  • Boxing Basics

    Stance, jab, cross, footwork and pad work with a partner. No sparring unless you ask for it.

    60 minutes, 14 people at most

  • Mobility and Core

    For backs stiff from the desk and tight hips. Low impact, and fine if you have not trained in years.

    40 minutes, 18 people at most

Before you come

What is in the 7-day trial?

The gym floor for seven days and any three group classes. No card and no joining fee.

I have never lifted before. Is that fine?

Yes. Every class has a cap, so the coach has time to teach you each movement from the start.

Is there a contract?

No. Monthly plans stop with 30 days notice. Yearly plans are paid up front and cost 20% less.

Is there a ladies-only time?

Yes, Tuesday and Thursday from 10 am to 12 pm, with a female coach on the floor.

Where do I park?

In the open car park behind the building. It is free for members.
