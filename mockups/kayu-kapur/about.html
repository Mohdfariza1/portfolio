About

A carpentry workshop that grew into a studio which designs and builds the whole room.

One team, from the drawing to the keys

Kayu & Kapur started in 2014 as a carpentry workshop in Petaling Jaya. Customers kept asking who would do the walls and the floor around our cabinets, so we learned to do those too.

Today the same people draw the room, build it and walk it with you at handover. Nothing is passed to a second contractor.

Who you will meet

  • Faris

    Designer

    Measures every house himself and draws each room in 3D.

  • Mei Ling

    Site supervisor

    On site every working day. She is the one who sends the evening photos.

  • Azman

    Workshop lead

    Builds the cabinets in Petaling Jaya before they reach your house.
