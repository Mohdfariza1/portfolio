How we work

Most clients come to us tired of not being told what is happening. We fixed that with a routine.

  1. First consultation

    Forty-five minutes with the partner who would handle your matter. You leave knowing your options and the likely cost.

  2. Written scope and fee

    Before any work starts, a letter sets out what we will do, what we will not, and the fee for each stage.

  3. An update every two weeks

    A short written update every fortnight, even when nothing has moved, so you never have to chase us.

  4. Closing and handover

    At the end you get the full file, the originals, and a plain note of anything you need to renew or remember.
